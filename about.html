About the Fellowship

Power Filled Believers Fellowship is an active, faith-based Christian ministry led by Apostle Aaron Mwansa. Though it is a growing local fellowship rather than a large denomination, it remains committed to spiritual growth, prayer, healing, and strong community fellowship.

Leadership

Apostle Aaron Mwansa oversees the spiritual direction of the ministry and serves as the primary teacher and minister.

Location

The fellowship is located in the Kadunga Area near Estermo, serving local residents and believers in the surrounding community.

Ministry Focus

Its ministries emphasize prayer, deliverance, anointing, healing, and spiritual empowerment for personal breakthrough.

Core Beliefs

  • The authority of Scripture as the infallible Word of God.
  • The power and presence of the Holy Spirit in everyday Christian life.
  • Spiritual deliverance, healing, and transformation through prayer and faith.
  • The importance of gathering in worship and fellowship for stronger spiritual growth.

Our Vision

To see lives transformed, communities strengthened, and believers empowered to walk in faith, power, and purpose through Jesus Christ.
