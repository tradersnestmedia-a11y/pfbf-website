✝️ Church Profile

Power Filled Believers Fellowship is an active, faith-based Christian ministry led by Apostle Aaron Mwansa. The fellowship is rooted in prayer, spiritual growth, and strong community fellowship.

Leadership

Pastor Aaron Mwansa oversees the ministry and serves as the primary teacher and spiritual leader.

Location

The fellowship is based in the Kadunga Area near Estermo and serves local believers and residents.

Ministry Focus

Known for prayer, healing, deliverance, and anointing services that strengthen faith and bring breakthrough.

🕊️ Core Beliefs

The fellowship is built on biblical truth, the power of the Holy Spirit, and the importance of gathering in worship and prayer.

Authority of Scripture

We believe the Bible is the infallible and authoritative Word of God.

Holy Spirit Power

We embrace the active presence of the Holy Spirit and the operation of spiritual gifts.

Healing & Deliverance

We believe in prayer for spiritual freedom, healing, and personal transformation.

🙌 Community & Fellowship

We believe spiritual growth is strengthened through regular gathering, worship, and collective prayer for the church and the community.
